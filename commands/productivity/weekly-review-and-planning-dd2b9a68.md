Run my weekly review. Ask me these one at a time and wait for each answer:

1. What did you finish this week? What slipped, and why?
2. What's on your calendar next week?
3. What are your top 3 goals for this {{period}}?

Then:
- Pick the 3 most important outcomes for next week.
- Time-block them into my calendar gaps (suggest specific slots).
- List things to delegate, defer or drop.
- Write a one-paragraph note to my future self for Friday.
