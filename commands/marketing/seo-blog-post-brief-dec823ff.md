Create an SEO content brief for the keyword "{{keyword}}" for {{site}}.

Include:
- Search intent and who is searching.
- Suggested title (under 60 chars) and meta description (under 155 chars).
- Outline with H2/H3s that fully answer the intent.
- Questions to answer (People Also Ask style).
- Internal links to add and external sources worth citing.
- What would make this better than the current top results.
- Target length and a clear CTA.
