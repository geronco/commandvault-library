Write a cold email to {{recipient_role}} at {{company_type}} about {{offer}}.

Rules:
- Under 120 words. One idea. One ask.
- First line is about them, not us.
- Specific proof (number or named customer) if available: {{proof}}.
- Low-friction CTA (a question, not "book a 30-minute call").

Also write two follow-ups (day 3 and day 8), each shorter than the last and adding something new. Give 3 subject lines under 6 words.
