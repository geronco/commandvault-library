Write landing page copy for {{product}}.

Audience: {{audience}}
Main pain: {{pain}}
Proof we have: {{proof}}

Deliver:
- 5 headline options (outcome-focused, under 10 words) + subheadline for the best one.
- Hero CTA button text (3 options).
- Sections: problem, how it works (3 steps), benefits (not features), social proof, objections & answers, final CTA.

Write like a person, not a brochure. No buzzwords ("revolutionary", "seamless", "unlock").
