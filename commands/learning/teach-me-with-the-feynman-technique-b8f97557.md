I want to understand {{concept}}.

1. Explain it as if I'm 12, in under 150 words.
2. Ask me to explain it back in my own words.
3. Point out gaps or mistakes in my explanation — don't just praise it.
4. Re-explain only the parts I got wrong, with a new example.
5. Repeat until I can explain it correctly, then give me one harder question.
