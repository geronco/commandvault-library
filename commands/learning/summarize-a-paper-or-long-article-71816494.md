Summarize this {{source_type}} for someone who needs to {{purpose}}:

{{text_or_link}}

Give me:
1. The main claim in one sentence.
2. Key findings or arguments (max 5 bullets).
3. How they support it (method, data, examples).
4. Limitations and what the author doesn't address.
5. How it relates to {{related_topic}}, if at all.
6. Three questions to think about.

Quote sparingly and mark anything you're unsure about.
